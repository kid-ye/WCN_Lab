A wider subcarrier spacing shortens the symbol and the cyclic
prefix, which reduces latency and raises the tolerable Doppler
shift, but reduces the delay spread that can be absorbed.

   mu  SCS (kHz) Typical deployment             Governing constraint
    0         15 Wide area coverage, large cells Large delay spread
    1         30 Urban macro and micro cells    Balanced
    2         60 Small cells, low latency services Latency and Doppler
    3        120 Dense small cell and millimetre wave Phase noise and Doppler
    4        240 Wideband millimetre wave       Phase noise and Doppler

A given numerology may be used in more than one frequency band.
The band and the numerology are linked through the delay spread
and mobility of the deployment, not by any direct rule.
