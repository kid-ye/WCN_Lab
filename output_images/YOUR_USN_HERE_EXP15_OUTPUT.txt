Minimum attenuation 0.1715 dB/km at 1.615 um
Rayleigh scattering varies as 1/lambda^4 and dominates below
1.3 um; infrared absorption rises beyond 1.6 um. The minimum
between them is the reason long haul systems work at 1.55 um.

At low received power the thermal noise of the receiver dominates
and the carrier to noise ratio rises 2 dB for every 1 dB of
optical power. At high power the relative intensity noise of the
source dominates and the carrier to noise ratio saturates.
